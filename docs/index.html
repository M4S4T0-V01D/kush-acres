What's growing

🌱

Grow from seed

Every plant grows in real time with its own look. Water it, beat the pests and mould, and harvest it ripe and frosty.

🧬

Breed & hunt phenos

Cross strains in the lab and pop seed packs looking for that one elite keeper. Keep her as a mother and clone her forever.

🏆

Win the Cup

Enter your best bud in the Cup and beat rivals like Skunk Patel and The Reverend for cash, Bucks and bragging rights.

🗺️

30 farms

Buy land in a redwood forest, a volcano island, a glowing neon valley, Zion Gardens, a walled fort, a haunted manor and more. Each one has its own map, town and strains.

🌧️

Weather & seasons

Sun, fog, rain and storms roll through on their own, and the seasons turn. Lamps glow and fireflies come out at night.

💨

Smoke a sesh

Roll joints, blunts and hotboxes from your own stash for buffs. Some trips get very trippy.

👨‍🌾

Hire a crew

Runners, fetchers, farmhands, cooks, a lookout and a manager keep the place running while you're away.

🏚️

Build it out

Grow rooms, drying sheds, trim rooms, rollers, presses, a kitchen, power, a deep bunker and a lawn full of decor.

🎉

Holidays

Real-calendar events: Halloween, Christmas, St Patrick's, Easter and of course 4/20, each with its own farm, tokens and pets.

📸

Photo mode

Pick the hour and the sky, frame your farm and save the shot.

🔄

4-way rotation

Spin the whole isometric world to see your farm from every side.

⬆️

Updates itself

Install once. The game checks GitHub for new releases and updates in a tap.
